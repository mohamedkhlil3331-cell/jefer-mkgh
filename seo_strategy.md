# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public Arabic company storefront and sign-in/registration experience for شركة جيفر التجارية, served by artifacts/erp-arabic.
- Guest-accessible product order pages (`/order/:id`) and public company assets.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated customer accounts, staff portals, administrative ERP screens, developer dashboard (`/mkgh`), design sandbox (`/__mockup`), and internal workshop/transport training slides and system guides.

## Target audience and topics
- Saudi customers seeking construction materials, cement supply, delivery, and transport services from Jefer Trading Company.
- Arabic is the primary public language. Source metadata declares https://jefer-mkgh.com/ as the canonical site; no live deployment verification is part of this scan.

## Crawler assumptions
- Public content should be findable and shareable, including by retrieval/AI crawlers.
- React/Vite client rendering is used; Google may render JavaScript, but social preview and most AI crawlers require initial HTML.

## Dismissed categories
- None recorded.
